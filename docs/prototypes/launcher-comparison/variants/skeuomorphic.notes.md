# Industrial depth

Source: [High Contrast Skeuomorphic Clean](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MengTo/Skills/main/agent-skills/web-design/high-contrast-skeuomorphic-clean/SKILL.md), read in full.

Applied rules: a pale neutral surround gives contrast to one charcoal housing; restrained warm red identifies active controls; nested modules use fine edges, inner shadows and top highlights. The tank artwork sits in an inset display. Edition switches are seated inside a recessed selector; Play and utility buttons have controlled bevels. Sans body type and small mono labels keep the material language precise. Hover brightness stays restrained, with no ambient motion.

Distinct structure: control bay beside a physically recessed artwork display, with a separate installation tray below. Small screens stack the display after the controls and retain every utility. Existing nodes are moved intact, preserving edition keyboard navigation, dialogs, Play and collection tools. The existing artwork URL resolves through the shell's base URL; no new assets or runtime requests are introduced.

Tradeoff: the pale outer housing and raised controls give strong material contrast but less cinematic edge-to-edge artwork. Phone layouts scroll to the artwork. No fake telemetry or ornamental data was added.

Verification: JavaScript syntax checked locally; integrated rendering, viewport checks and interaction checks are handled by the comparison owner. No baseline or production files changed.
